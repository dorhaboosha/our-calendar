# Acceptance criteria: where each one is checked

The 22 criteria of spec section 14, each with the automated tests that check it and the
check on the live site. Gone through on **2026-11-28**, at the end of Phase 5.

- **Unit:** `src/**/*.test.ts`. **Integration:** `tests/integration` (a real Postgres,
  and a fake Google, [`fake-google.ts`](../tests/integration/fake-google.ts)).
  **E2E:** `tests/e2e` (Playwright, the production build, desktop and phone).
- **Live** checks are done on https://our-calendar-ay05.onrender.com with the real
  Google accounts after each phase is deployed. The Phase 5 live check is below.

## Phase 5 live check

Done on **2026-11-28**, after merging, on the live site. Both users have iPhones, so
Android wasn't checked on a phone (its manifest and icons are covered by `pwa.spec.ts`).

1. **Installed on both iPhones** (Safari, Share > Add to Home Screen): named
   OurCalendar, with the logo, and it opens without Safari's bars. Passed.
2. **Return and connection, on the iPhones:** an event added on one phone shows on the
   other when coming back to the app, without reloading, and an open form keeps its
   text. In airplane mode the notice shows and saving fails with its message, keeping
   the form; once the connection is back the notice goes and nothing is saved on its
   own. Passed.
3. **One occurrence changed in Google:** a daily series of five; in Google one
   occurrence was edited (title and time) and another deleted ("This event" only).
   "Export again" set both back, one copy per day, nothing left over. Then the series'
   title was changed in the app, and every occurrence took it, the two set back too.
   Deleting the series in the app removed it from Google. Passed.
4. **Timing:** a new event reached Google in under 20 seconds, a change to the series
   and its deletion in about 25 seconds. "Export again" with every event took
   **4 seconds** (about 2.5 minutes in Phase 4, when events were sent one at a time).
   "N events left" didn't show: it counts the per-event jobs, and they were all done
   before the settings page's first reload, 3 seconds later. It shows when there are
   many events (`google-export.spec.ts`).
5. **Consent screen:** the app is renamed OurCalendar on Google's consent screen
   (Branding, name only). Google asked for no verification, and the publishing status
   didn't change.
6. **Still to check, a few days after merging:** the scheduled export runs at 7 and 37
   minutes past, and how often it really runs (see
   [operations](operations.md#measured-use)); Neon's compute sleeps between runs.
